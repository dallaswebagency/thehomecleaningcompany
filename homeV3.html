Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Call Sonia and she will help you directly.

(403) 968-5006
Do I need to supply cleaning products?

For regular house cleaning, rates cover labour only and we use your materials and equipment, so your preferred products are used, especially where health and safety matter. On request we can bring supplies on a bill-back basis. Move in / move out cleans include all supplies and equipment.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every cleaner is individually bonded, security checked and insured. We carry $2,000,000 comprehensive liability insurance and a cleaning fidelity bond, and all employees are covered by WCB.

Will I get the same cleaner each time?

Yes. You can interview a cleaner who's the right match and they'll come every week. In case of illness or holidays, a different worker is allocated.

What if my home is larger than 1,600 sq ft?

Houses larger than 1,600 sq ft are subject to additional charges. Tell us your home size when you book and we'll confirm the price.

Do I have to be home during the clean?

No. We secure your keys, so you don't have to worry. Regular clients are invoiced monthly.

Are you pet friendly?

Absolutely. Many of our long-time clients have pet-filled homes, and our carpet steam cleaning uses a non-toxic solution that's safe for pets.
